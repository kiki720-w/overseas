NASA imagery notice

This revision replaces the previous Three.js example textures, whose original imagery provenance had not been verified, with unmodified downloads from NASA's official Earth Observatory image archive. No prior Three.js day/night/cloud/normal texture is included in the revised production bundle.

Earth day: NASA/GSFC Blue Marble, land/ocean/ice/clouds, 2002.
Cloud map: NASA/GSFC Blue Marble Clouds, 2002.
Blue Marble data credit: Reto Stockli, NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center.

https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/earth-matters/2011/10/06/crafting-the-blue-marble/

Credit NASA and the named data providers. These images are used as geographic visualization; they are not live satellite data, business evidence or a NASA endorsement. NASA marks are not used.

No downloaded image file is resized, recolored or re-encoded. The WebGL display shader applies a muted blue tint and a city-light luminance mask to preserve the selected visual treatment. The globe geometry, atmosphere and country markers are application rendering, not modifications to the source files.
